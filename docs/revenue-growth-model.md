# Revenue Growth Model - claude-production-patterns

Updated: 2026-05-30

This note translates the archived proof into a current, ethical revenue path. It keeps the repository's archived status visible while preserving the commercial idea behind the work.

## Model

| Field | Decision |
|---|---|
| Revenue type | Archived proof to diagnostic, workshop, enablement, pilot, or partner package |
| Primary reader or buyer | AI platform leaders, staff engineers, governance owners, and teams moving from prototype to production. |
| Wedge | Production teams need cost controls, prompt versioning, eval gates, canary rollout, and audit trails before scale. |
| Operating note | Sell the revived workflow or advisory package; do not sell an unsupported archive as a live product. |

## Offer Ladder

| Offer | Price assumption | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Production-readiness audit | $8k-$25k | Score a Claude stack against cost, reliability, eval, and compliance controls. |
| Operating-pattern implementation | $35k-$120k | Install selected controls into a shared platform or product service. |
| Managed governance retainer | $8k-$30k/month | Maintain eval gates, dashboards, prompt registry, and release reviews. |

## Activation Loop

- Traffic source: Prototype-to-production checklists.
- First action: read the README status note and one proof path that can be checked quickly.
- Conversion offer: Production-readiness audit.
- Expansion: convert a diagnostic or workshop into a scoped pilot, enablement pack, or maintained partner asset.

## Growth Experiments

- Publish the readiness scorecard
- Record a cost-control and canary walkthrough
- Offer the audit as the entry product

## Metrics

- Audit starts
- Controls implemented
- Eval pass rate
- Cost anomaly reduction

## Ethical Boundary

- Avoid promising exact cost reduction without customer telemetry
- Treat compliance examples as implementation aids, not legal advice
- Keep vendor-specific claims neutral
- Keep archived status visible in sales or portfolio conversations.
- Do not invent users, revenue, endorsements, certifications, or customer traction.
- Use buyer psychology only to reduce confusion and risk, not to pressure users.
