# Monetization Playbook - claude-production-patterns

This is a commercial planning artifact, not a revenue guarantee. It exists to turn an archived technical proof into a clear, ethical selling motion.

## Commercial Lane

- **Lane:** Claude production-readiness operating kit
- **Primary buyer:** AI platform leaders, staff engineers, governance owners, and teams moving from prototype to production.
- **Why this can sell:** Production teams need cost controls, prompt versioning, eval gates, canary rollout, and audit trails before scale.

## Offer Ladder

1. **Production-readiness audit** - $8k-$25k. Score a Claude stack against cost, reliability, eval, and compliance controls.
2. **Operating-pattern implementation** - $35k-$120k. Install selected controls into a shared platform or product service.
3. **Managed governance retainer** - $8k-$30k/month. Maintain eval gates, dashboards, prompt registry, and release reviews.

## Acquisition Channels

- Prototype-to-production checklists
- CTO and platform engineering outreach
- Companion demos from agent-runtime repos

## Proof Gates Before Selling

- Patterns compile and test
- Each pattern has a crisp failure mode
- Architecture docs point to validation hooks

## Risk Boundaries

- Avoid promising exact cost reduction without customer telemetry
- Treat compliance examples as implementation aids, not legal advice
- Keep vendor-specific claims neutral

## 30/60/90 Day GTM

- **30 days:** Publish the readiness scorecard
- **60 days:** Record a cost-control and canary walkthrough
- **90 days:** Offer the audit as the entry product

## Metrics That Matter

- Audit starts
- Controls implemented
- Eval pass rate
- Cost anomaly reduction

## Positioning Note

Keep the repository's archived status visible. Sell the reusable workflow, diagnostic, enablement material, or pilot path that the proof makes credible; do not sell the archive as an unsupported production service.
